ELECTRICAL CIRCUITS WITHIN THE HOUSE

House has two circuits:

  • 15 A - high power appliances (geyser, iron, AC)
  • 5 A - low power appliances (bulbs)

Common domestic circuit: Live and neutral wires come from outside → main fuse → electricity meter → distribution box → sockets (parallel).

Three-pin socket: live, neutral, and earth (top pin to ground).

Each appliance has its own ON/OFF switch. Appliances are connected in parallel so each gets equal voltage and one switch does not affect others.

Each electrical appliance connected in parallel has an independent path for current and it works at the same voltage, that is 220 V.

Role of earth wire

Earth wire has green insulation. One end goes to a metal plate deep underground; other end to the metal body of appliances (iron, toaster, fridge).

If current leaks to the metal body, earth wire gives a low-resistance path into the ground, so the person does not get a shock.

SAFETY COMPONENTS

Short circuit

Live and neutral wires touch directly (damaged insulation/fault). Current rises a lot → heat, sparks, fire.

Overloading

Too many appliances on one plug. Wires heat up, insulation may melt, and short circuit can happen. Safety devices are needed.

Electric Fuse

Safety device that stops undesirably high current from voltage fluctuation or fault, so wires/appliances do not overheat or catch fire.

Structure

  • Porcelain casing: hollow block with brass terminals T1 and T2; live wire to T1; no direct link between T1 and T2.
  • Porcelain grip: solid block with copper clamps and fuse wire between them. When inserted, current flows T1 → fuse wire → T2.

If current is too high, fuse wire melts and current stops.

Features

Made of metal alloy (lead and tin). Melting point about 200°C. Connected in series in the live wire where current enters.

When heat exceeds safe limit, fuse melts and breaks the circuit. Found near main switch in porcelain cartridge.

Higher rating = thicker wire. Ratings: 1 A, 2 A, 3 A, 5 A, 10 A, etc.

Miniature Circuit Breaker (MCB)

MCBs are replacing fuses. Fuse problem: melted wire must be replaced. MCB trips automatically; just switch it ON again to restore supply.

Some MCBs can resume supply automatically. Always look for ISI mark on MCBs.

Remember

Buy only appliances with ISI mark. ISI = Indian Standard Institute (Bureau of Indian Standard). It means safe, less energy waste, and good quality.

LIGHTNING SAFETY

Lightning starts from friction of water particles in clouds. Negative charge at bottom, positive at top; ground becomes positive. When charge is large, air cannot resist — charges meet. This electric discharge is lightning (with heat, light, sound).

It can happen cloud-to-ground, cloud-to-cloud, or inside the same cloud.

Lightning is dangerous. It often strikes tall buildings and trees. No open place is safe. When thunder roars, go indoors.

  • Hearing thunder = rush to a safer place.
  • After lightning flash, count slowly: every 3 counts ≈ 1 km away.
  • Flash and thunder together = storm is above you.
  • After last thunder, wait 30 minutes before coming out.

A building or house is safe. Remain inside.

Do's and Don'ts during a Thunderstorm — Outside the House

  • Open vehicles (motorbikes, tractors, open cars) are not safe.
  • Open fields, tall trees, park shelters, elevated places do not protect us.
  • Carrying umbrella is not a good idea.
